Welcome to Space Station 14! You have just started your shift aboard a NanoTrasen space station and along with your crewmates should cooperate, perform your job role to the best of your ability, and have fun (ideally not at the expensee of others).

Please make sure you understand the server rules (F1). If you have any questions, you can ask your colleagues on shift in person or over radio, or your fellow players via Discord. You can also ask admins for help (AHelp) by hitting ESC > Admin Help and typing a message. Especially do this if you witness someone breaking rules.

To reiterate, you are all a crew of people who work together (even the clown) and you should not be harming each other unless you have an extremely good reason. Every member of the crew fills a different niche– try to work with and not over or against your coworkers.
