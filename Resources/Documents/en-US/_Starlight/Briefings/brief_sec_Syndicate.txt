[bold]CENTRAL SECURITY DIVISION – SECURE BRIEFING[/bold]
[mono][bold]Subject:[/bold] The Syndicate

The information contained within this document is restricted to Security personnel and should be issued to officers on a need-to-know basis.

The details within will cover standard modus operandi of the potential threat, as well as operating procedures in relation to and countermeasures in case of hostilities with the aforementioned threat.
═════════════════════════════════════════════
[bold]Preface[/bold]
It is no secret that NanoTrasen, and by extension her subordinates and allies, have many enemies both corporate and extrajudicial. It is also no secret that less than a century ago, a major corporate conflict was fought between all these parties in which NanoTrasen emerged victorious. What is perhaps lesser known, however, is the reality that the war never truly ended– it has merely gone cold.

[bold]Briefing[/bold]
The Syndicate is a loose conglomerate of NanoTrasen's enemies, comprising corporations, interest groups, cult sects, radical activists, and glorified pirates. They are united by their rallying cry and shared goal: "Death to  NanoTrasen".

Syndicate assets come in many forms and faces. Common aggressors include infiltrated agents, swayed contractors, and civilians employed to facilitate tactical operations. However, there has also been an alarming increasing frequency in armed terror attacks by the Gorlex Marauder arm of their coalition, the results of which involve rivers of pointlessly spilled blood and emptied cartridges.

The Syndicate continues to grow bolder with every passing day, but the strength to resist their aggression follows suit. We will not bend. We will not break. We will not yield. 

Glory to NanoTrasen.

[bold]Procedures[/bold]
All Syndicate assets are humanoids whose subterfuge is conducted through conventional and generally mundane means. There are no protocols for containing conventional Syndicate agents besides adherence to standard operating procedures, although operatives infiltrating from deep space rather than via civilian ranks tend to be heavily armed and beyond negotiating with. 

Keep in mind that any individual who formally declares themselves a Syndicate Operative (especially if they adopt the moniker of "Nuclear Operative") is to be considered an Enemy of the Corporation under section 5-10 of NanoTrasen corporate law, and either detained and transferred to Central Command holding cells following crew change or executed outright provided the levying two or more other capital crimes.

[bold]Countermeasures[/bold]
Simple deterrence against Syndicate activity involves having a well organized security department and a competent commanding team. Officers should seek to ensure the safety and productivity of station personnel and integrity of assets, while heads should work to keep operations coordinated and smooth. In the case of breaches, investigations and swift application of justice should be applied as with any other criminal activity.

Countermeasures against Syndicate Operatives is even simpler: overwhelming, unrelenting, superior firepower. They are not here to negotiate, so do not bother with pleasantries or non-lethal options. Turn every operative who dares to strike one of our stations into an example for the next one.

[bold]Glory to NanoTrasen.[/bold]
