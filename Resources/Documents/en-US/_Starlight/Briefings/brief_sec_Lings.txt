[bold]CENTRAL SECURITY DIVISION – SECURE BRIEFING[/bold]
[mono][bold]Subject:[/bold] Changelings

The information contained within this document is restricted to Security personnel and should be issued to officers on a need-to-know basis. 

The details within will cover standard modus operandi of the potential threat, as well as operating procedures in relation to and countermeasures in case of hostilities with the aforementioned threat.
═════════════════════════════════════════════
[bold]Preface[/bold]
The details of this brief concern a threat profile which matches that of loyal members of the sectoral workforce. Use discretion and reason in identification and when considering procedural responses, and avoid alarmist tendencies regarding "shapeshifting monsters" from permeating into the general consciousness of station crew wherever possible.

[bold]Briefing[/bold]
Changelings are a highly intelligent species of shapeshifters which inhabit much of the known galaxy in small numbers. Often falling squarely on either side of the sociability spectrum, most members of the species pass in normal society without drawing attention or create bases of power within fervent or insular social circles and organizations. Regardless of their choice of disguise, changelings remain united as a species by their unique need to consume raw biomass as opposed to the biomolecular focused diets of virtually all other species known. This need has led to their infamy in certain sectors as flesh-eaters and dangerous predators whose identification is cause for immediate elimination.

In reality, the psyche and motivations of each individual changeling are as varied as that of any other species, and this sector's uniquely clean slate has allowed for an unsteady peace to exist where it would be unthinkable in any other. This is not a treaty sealed in ink and blood however– out of malice, misunderstanding, fear, or simply base hunger, attacks against civilian and military personnel by changelings does still occur, however infrequently. This is why this document exists in spite of a supposed coexistence.

[bold]Procedures[/bold]
The procedures for deterring, containing, or, if required, eliminating a changeling or similar threat is as follows.

1. Pre-identification of changelings involves the discovery of any given corpse with a visibly hollowed appearance, typically found half-digested in or near a pool of acid (although crafty suspects may take measures to cover their tracks). Corpses confirmed by coroning to be unnaturally hollowed serve as immediate confirmation of a changeling aboard. 

2. Identification of an active changeling aboard a NanoTrasen Deep-Space Installation may escalate into an undesirable paranoid mass screening, which benefits no parties and creates an immediate air of distrust among station crews. Refrain from such wide-net tests unless absolutely necessary to identify an elusive active threat, and instead use forensics to track any traces to and from the hollowed body. This includes DNA residue on the body itself, surrounding acid pools, and logs of nearby airlocks.

3. It may be necessary to communicate to the changeling privately in regards to its food situation, although one should not attempt such unarmed in case of being led by a ruse. An ideal situation sees a diplomatic solution achieved without the crew at large ever needing to learn the true nature of their coworker.

4. If an agreement cannot be reached or any attempts result in immediate hostiliy, then disarmament and non-lethal engagement is unlikely to be advisable. Changelings are extremely physically powerful, are capable of secreting a blinding acid that can corrode restraints and confuse attackers, and possess an extremely large variety of defensive adaptations the true extent of which is still not fully known even following extensive analysis and dissection. If you must engage, keep your distance and do not do so alone.

[bold]Countermeasures[/bold]
Changelings are capable of tearing anything within arm's reach to shreds within moments. Do not allow civilians or your personnel to get close if an active altercation is ongoing for threat of death and dismemberment.

In terms of neutralizing power, incendiary weapons are extremely effective at warding off and discouraging further aggression from most changelings. Conventional ballistics and energy weapons tend to be less effective towards this means, which is hypothesized to be due to the lack of animalistic instinct dedicated to avoiding these weapons.

Changelings' hunger poses a major angle  potential weakness for them, as they will be weakened drastically by starvation within less than an hour of being cut off from food sources. It is not uncommon for isolated changelings to starve to death or be cut down after a desperate starved attack.  

If they have recently fed, changelings are able to enter a death-like stasis at will in which they will slowly regenerate from wounds. In this state, they are indistinguishable from a corpse, but they remain very much aware of their surrounds and are not indisposed to resuming an attack upon their self-revival. 

Due to this inability to contain or kill them through conventional means, a hostile changeling who cannot be dissuaded or driven off through any means should be reported to Central Command immediately, and potentially cremated if the threat posed is great enough. Such a step should only be taken as a last resort, when all other avenues of de-escalation have been exhausted.
