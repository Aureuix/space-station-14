[bold]CENTRAL SECURITY DIVISION – SECURE BRIEFING[/bold]
[mono][bold]Subject:[/bold] The Lighthouse

The information contained within this document is restricted to Security personnel and should be issued to officers on a need-to-know basis.

The details within will cover standard modus operandi of the potential threat, as well as operating procedures in relation to and countermeasures in case of hostilities with the aforementioned threat.
═════════════════════════════════════════════
[bold]Preface[/bold]
Bright-Eyes (sometimes spelled "Brighteyes") are a cultural group of the Shadekin, an extraplanar species whose socially outcast members often find refuge among NanoTrasen's workforce. The term "Bright-Eyes" refers to their luminescent eyes, an identifier which indicates them as "unburnt" (referring to a still poorly-understood ritual in which Kin either ascend or are cast out for failing). While virtually all Shadekin under NanoTrasen employ are some degree of burnt, Bright-Eyes have full retention of their extraplanar spark, enabling them to use a large host of powers including but not limited to interplanar transit, supernatural immobilixation, and the creation of darkness.

Most Bright-Eyes are not hostile and typically can be negotiated with for great mutual gain, especially in knowledge, or deterred from any altercations with crew through simple visual messaging. Such Brighteyes are to be treated as visitors should they make their presences known, although there should be no expectation of understanding in regards to paperwork or ID cards. Unfortunately, not all members of this culture are as peaceful.

[bold]Briefing[/bold]
"The Lighthouse" is an organization ofradical extremist Shadekin which has recently made its presence in the sector known through multiple lethal incidents aboard NanoTrasen stations and in nearby space. Seemingly headed by an enigmatic cult-adjacent figure identified as "Progress", with a large caste of warriors and scholars both, The Lighthouse appears to be intent on pursuing a doctrine of attempting to restore burnt peers' Brightness through whatever means necessary, a process they call "Rekindling".

The Lighthouse appears to be quasi-religious in nature and structure, with even its leader beholden to an as-of-yet unidentified "Reverend Flame". Despite their apparently noble goals, they have seen little issue with excessive  violence, even against their own peers. Current analysis suggests that their operation is very much akin to a cult, complete with a general intolerance for divergent viewpoints within their field of focus.

This is a dangerous enemy organization whose members should not be underestimated. If Lighthouse members make their presence known or are otherwise identified aboard a Spectral Sector Deep-Space Installation, all security forces are to immediately prepare and distribute countermeasures in coordination with research and development personnel.

[bold]Procedures[/bold]
The procedures for deterring, containing, or, if required, eliminating a Lighthouse member or other hostile Bright-Eyes is as follows.

1. A positive identification threshold must be met. Either through self-identification, rhetoric and behavior, or recognition by crew, the allegiance of the suspect must be established beyond reasonable doubt before moving to further escalation.

2. In the case of a positive identification, any mining personnel are to immediately refocus efforts on procurement of raw bluespace crystals, and research should be covertly refocused to experimental paths. Be aware that communications and accesses are likely to be compromised at this stage due to the suspect's innate ability to traverse between realspace and nullspace at will.

3. Any key sensitive equipment should be secured immediately within the station Vault or Armory, or within the radius of a protection or nullspace anchor. Any at-risk personnel should also be provided with at least one refined bluespace crystal for personal defense, and nullspace drainers should be distributed if made available.

4. Cooperate with research personnel to locate, secure, and potentially destroy any rifts to their home dimension, identifiable by their droning tone, pulsing darkness, and light suppression. This can be done using standard anomaly containment equipment such as an Anomalous Particle Emitter. Destroying a rift will also yield a bluespace crystal, which should be immediately applied to continue containment efforts.

5. Respond to distress calls expeditiously. Bright-Eyes are capable guerillas, a strength heavily played into by Lighthouse fighters. Do not give them the chance to wreak a campaign of terror. Use countermeasures as listed in the next section to contain and, if necessary, neutralize the suspect.

6. Failing all else, report the situation directly to Central Command and await a response. You may be required to conduct a retaliatory expedition into the suspect's home dimension in order to destroy their base of operations and force their retreat.

[bold]Countermeasures[/bold]
All Shadekin are negatively affected by the energy released from a bluespace crystal's collapse, but the effect is especially prevalent among the Bright-Eyed population. Destruction of a crystal within a 5-meter radius of one will forcibly drag them into realspace if they are between planes, as well as disorienting them and causing genetic deformation. These are your most powerful weapon against them, particularly if paired with the element of surprise.

Nullspace experimentation in the previous decade has also created nullspace anchors and drainers which are capable of saturating an area with a suppressive field when powered. These can be deployed or worn to create both static and mobile safe-zones, and should be used to secure at-risk personnel and assets where possible.

Central Command is able to procure a small amount of supporting equipment for combatting suspects who cannot be easily drawn out of nullspace. Self-procurement on station is highly inadvisable due to the components required in manufacturing. These include the nullspace goggles and, if necessary, a catalyst for an interplanar penetrator known as a Dark Breacher, for use in clause 6 of the Procedures subheading.

Bright-Eyes are intelligent and fully capable of understanding and utilizing station weapons. Do not under any circumstances allow the protection anchor within the Armory to fail. Likewise, secure any easily weaponized tools and armor as soon as a threat is made evident.  Furthermore, as they are able to phase out of any and all restraints, any attempt to imprison one must involve liberal usage of nullspace-anchoring technology, the lack of which would ensure such an operation impossible. If an arrest cannot be made, an elimination should be considered.

Glory to NanoTrasen.
